Matching method: `backend/cross_dataset_overlap.py` (band-passed 100–1,000 Hz at 4 kHz, normalized
cross-correlation of every clip against every reference recording). Threshold 0.75 sits in a clean gap
of the score distribution for both overlaps.

## 3. Label harmonization

ICBHI → 5 classes: COPD → copd, Healthy → healthy, Pneumonia → pneumonia, Asthma → asthma,
URTI/Bronchiectasis/Bronchiolitis/LRTI → **bronchial** (a heterogeneous group). KAUH: N → healthy,
Asthma, Pneumonia, COPD, BRON → bronchial; heart failure, lung fibrosis, pleural effusion and combined
diagnoses → out of label (external abstention test only). Kaggle: folder names. **Risk:** "healthy" in
KAUH ("N") and ICBHI may not be defined identically; the bronchial group mixes acute and chronic
conditions.

**Confound.** All COPD audio is ICBHI (646 of 793 on the AKG C417L, a device that records no other
class). Before round 2b every KAUH-recorded training clip (the 316 Kaggle cuts) was asthma, bronchial
or pneumonia; round 2b adds 21 KAUH healthy/COPD patients. ICBHI healthy and bronchial recordings are
all Meditron. Any model can therefore partly predict the class from the recording source.

## 5. Under-represented classes and missing metadata

- ICBHI asthma: 1 patient; LRTI: 2 (merged into bronchial). Asthma in the cohort is almost entirely
  Kaggle/KAUH-derived.
- Pneumonia: 6 ICBHI patients; the rest Kaggle.
- No demographics are used (ICBHI demographic files exist; KAUH file names carry age/sex; Kaggle has
  none). No device metadata for Kaggle clips of unknown origin. Chest location is not used.
- Each KAUH recording exists three times (bell, diaphragm, extended filters) — always grouped by
  patient, never split across partitions.

## 6. External holdout

`backend/data/kauh_external_holdout_manifest.csv`: 51 patients (18 healthy, 5 COPD, 1 bronchial,
27 out of label), selected by `kauh.py --split-for-training` (seeded shuffle per class). Its
predecessor, the 72-patient `kauh_external_manifest.csv`, was used for EXP-011 and EXP-013.
`experiments/external.py` refuses any patient present in the evaluated model's own split.
